01 — THE CONCEPT

What is an Autonomous Workspace?

An autonomous workspace combines AI agents, intelligent tools, automation and infrastructure to perform meaningful work with less human intervention.

Instead of using AI as a single assistant, autonomous workspaces connect multiple systems that can research, analyze, communicate, execute tasks and work together.
